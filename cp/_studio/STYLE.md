# Club Pilates Sippy Downs: social style guide

The design kit for @clubpilatessippy. Our positioning: a fully equipped Pilates studio (reformer, Wunda chair, springboard), not just another reformer studio. It sits inside the 2026 Club Pilates Brand Book (franchisor's rules always win) and gives the studio a calmer, more editorial local feel than the national templates.

## Brand rules we never break (from the brand book)

- The Club Pilates logo is always the supplied artwork: blue (CP Brand Blue) on light backgrounds, white on blue, slate or photos. Never stretched, recoloured, redrawn or typeset.
- "Sippy Downs" sits as a separate small label under the logo, never inside it.
- The mandala may be used as a watermark (low opacity, cropped off the edge).
- The trademarked tagline is never typeset by us.
- Only brand palette colours.

## Type

Jost, as the free stand-in for the brand's Venti CF (licensed). If head office supplies Venti CF files, swap them into render.py.

- Headline: Jost Medium, with an optional Light second half (`<span class='lt'>`), e.g. "Never been on a reformer? *Good. Start here.*"
- Eyebrow: Jost Medium, capitals, very wide tracking, like the national grid.
- Body: Jost Light.
- No italics, no script fonts, no emojis on images.

## Layouts (render.py kinds)

- `photo`: full-bleed studio photo, slate shade at the bottom for text, soft shade at the top for the logo.
- `split`: photo above (or below, for stories) a Light Tan, Light Blue, blue or slate panel.
- `text-ltan`, `text-light`, `text-blue`, `text-slate`, `text-white`: type-led cards with the mandala watermark.
- `quote`: weekly quote card, blue by default.
- `logo`: end frame for reels.
- Sizes: feed 1080x1350, square 1080x1080, stories 1080x1920.

## Photography

Only the studio's own photos (2025 photoshoot, in the Sippy Downs Google Drive). Bright, real classes, real members, real instructors. Never stock, never AI-generated people. Never the same cover photo twice in a row. Never put text over a face.

## Voice

The brand book's voice (warm, empowering, positive, inclusive, knowledgeable, supportive) in Nichola's register: conversational, British spelling, short sentences, educational rather than salesy, a little humour. No exclamation marks, no emojis, no em dashes, no motivational clichés, no "transform", no body-shape promises, no injury or medical claims.

"Free intro class" is used, because it is the national offer's name.

## Content mix (per week)

1. First-timers: what the intro class is, what to bring, what to expect.
2. Education: levels, class formats (Restore is myofascial release), the equipment beyond the reformer (Wunda chair, springboard), a cue explained. At least one post or story a week shows we're fully equipped.
3. Instructors and team: training, craft, personalities (first names only with their OK).
4. Community: members (no names unless they've agreed), milestones, awards, studio life.
5. Quote card (Saturday).

Never two posts on the same topic in one week.
