# Club Pilates Sippy Downs: free intro and three-for-$25 strategy

Prepared 11 November 2026 with the club-pilates-meta-strategist skill. Nothing has been published, switched on or changed in the ad account.

## 1. Recommendation and commercial reason

**Run the free intro class as the one always-on campaign at A$35 a day, and hold the three-for-$25 pack until it has real terms and a working purchase route.**

- **The free intro is the right engine.** It removes the biggest barrier we can see, not knowing what happens in a first class. It's the franchisor's national offer. The close happens in person, at the intro. Its CAP route into ClubReady becomes usable once a test lead is seen in the correct record.
- **A$35 a day keeps us under the ceiling with room to spare.** A$35 plus about A$10 for the live $99 offer is A$45 a day, about A$1,395 in a 31-day month, leaving roughly A$100 of buffer. The buffer matters: the $99 figure is an estimate, Meta can overspend a daily budget on some days, and four unexplained ads are running on our Page. The A$80 a day draft built yesterday (about A$2,430 a month) breaks the A$1,500 ceiling.
- **The three-for-$25 pack shouldn't launch yet.** It has no terms, and no ClubReady package or checkout that I can find. It would split a small budget three ways and compete with the $99 trial for the same buyer. The 4-for-$40 and 5-for-$50 forms show no leads, so their ad results can't be judged from the forms. Your own read is that those promos converted poorly to membership. Build it properly, then test it in place of the $99 offer, not on top of it.

**The honest volume maths.** A$35 a day is about A$1,085 a month on the free intro. At our account average (about A$45 a lead) that's about 24 leads. At our best single ad's rate (about A$25) it's about 43. Your goal of 100 to 140 leads a month isn't reachable inside A$1,500 at any cost per lead we've seen. It would need about A$11 to A$15 a lead, or roughly A$2,500 to A$6,300 a month at our real costs.

My recommendation is to keep A$1,500 for now and fix the steps after the lead: call speed, booking and attendance. Then scale on cost per attended intro. A lead who doesn't turn up costs the same as one who does.

## 7. Test plan, measurement and budget

- **Hypothesis:** showing live coaching, in the first class and every class after, will produce more attended intros per dollar than our past generic studio images. It answers the "will I do it right?" worry better than a price or a photo.
- **Design:** operational delivery across four ads, not a controlled test. Meta will favour some ads, and that's delivery, not proof.
- **Primary outcome:** cost per attended intro in ClubReady. Then intro-to-member rate.
- **Before launch:** pull the last 90 days of LASER numbers from ClubReady as the baseline (leads, appointments, shows, enrolments).
- **Reading the results:**
  - **Week 4:** a directional read only. Just weeks 1 and 2 are mature by then.
  - **Week 8:** the decision read.
- **Provisional economics:** the minimum 3-month membership brings in about A$442 to A$962. Media cost per new member should sit well below that before we scale. We'll set the real limit together from contribution and retention.
- **Tracking:**
  - Tyson records contacted, booked, attended and joined for every Meta lead.
  - We confirm whether ClubReady can show which ad a lead came from.
  - If it can't, we match Meta's lead export to ClubReady by count.
- **Offer B:** once its terms and route exist, test it in place of the $99 offer at a similar budget. This needs your yes, because it replaces a live campaign. January will be distorted by competitors' "until 2027" promotions ending, so compare cohorts carefully and treat the result as directional.
- **Budget:** A$35 a day for the free intro, plus about A$10 for the $99 offer, is about A$1,395 in a 31-day month. If you approve it, I'd set an account spending limit as a hard cap.
