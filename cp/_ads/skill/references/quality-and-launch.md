## Critic pass

After drafting, run a separate critic pass. If an agent or subagent tool is available, give an independent reviewer the brief, this file and the actual output (copy, images, settings), with no hint of the verdict you want. Otherwise review it yourself as a sceptical outsider, in a separate step after drafting.

Questions:

1. Is the customer and motivation specific enough to guide a decision?
2. Does the hook give that person a reason to stop?
3. Is the promised benefit believable and supported by the claims register?
4. Does the ad fit this offer and this stage of readiness?
5. Could the copy belong to any Pilates studio?
6. Does the visual show the message, or undermine it?
7. Are qualifications, reviews, award wording, prices and terms exactly right?
8. Is there one clear next action, and does the destination deliver it?
9. Is the form, checkout or booking coherent on a phone?
10. Any policy, personal-attribute or misleading-claim issue?
11. Can the studio actually fulfil the promise and follow up the volume?
12. For weak results: is there a better explanation than "the creative is bad" (offer, form, follow-up speed, tracking, delivery, budget)?

Fix material issues before presenting. Remove or replace unsupported claims with substantiated wording.

## Launch blockers vs evidence limits

**Blockers (stop launch):** wrong Page, Instagram account or ad account; missing authority for the action; unverified material offer terms; broken or wrong conversion route (paid offer in a free form, form not reaching ClubReady); a false or unsupported claim; a personal-attribute or health policy breach; budget that breaks the monthly ceiling.

**Evidence limits (note, don't stop):** competitor ads not visible; no benchmark; small sample; missing optional data.

Never mark an asset as visually checked unless you looked at it, or a journey as working unless you observed the downstream result.

## Journey trace

Ad -> destination -> genuine lead or purchase -> correct ClubReady record -> Tyson's follow-up -> booking -> attendance -> membership.

Check each link separately. Lead delivery into ClubReady and CRM outcome feedback to Meta (Conversions API for CRM) are different capabilities. Don't infer Pixel health, purchases or membership signals from a CRM connection. The national landing page (lp.clubpilates.com.au) cannot carry our own pixel; the franchisor shares its pixel only on request.

## Reporting the review

Show a short **"What I challenged or corrected"** list: real issues found and how they were fixed. If nothing material was found, say so. No self-scores, no manufactured criticisms.
